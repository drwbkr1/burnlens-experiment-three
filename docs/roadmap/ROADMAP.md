# BurnLens Experiment Three Roadmap

## Terminal outcome

Experiment Three will build and truthfully close one deliberately bounded
neural change-detection experiment. Completion requires a verified,
fresh-process-replayable lifecycle. It does not require favorable comparative
metrics.

The primary question is whether a 137-parameter pointwise neural detector can
avoid the canonical U-Net's constant-class behavior on the frozen Experiment 1
Deschutes retrospective compatibility benchmark.

Each cycle begins by reconciling repository, custody, runtime, model,
evaluation, release, and public-state truth. It then selects one bounded,
highest-leverage evidence unit from the first incomplete milestone.

## Milestone 2 - Synthetic lifecycle preflight

**State:** Pending.

**Deliverables**

- Implement the six-channel, 137-parameter pointwise neural detector.
- Use synthetic inputs and disposable weights to validate shapes, masks,
  forward and backward passes, finite loss, nonzero gradients, optimizer steps,
  changed weight hashes, known-rule loss reduction, deterministic inference,
  safe checkpoint reconstruction, and identical reconstructed logits.
- Validate metric fixtures and geospatial write/reopen behavior without opening
  evaluation evidence.

**Exit gate**

- The synthetic lifecycle runs from one documented command in a pinned local
  runtime and produces a hash-bound report.
- Preflight weights are marked disposable and cannot enter substantive runs.

## Milestone 3 - Protocol freeze

**State:** Pending; substantive training is not authorized before this gate.

**Deliverables**

- Freeze the architecture and exact parameter audit.
- Bind the already approved architecture, loss, optimizer, learning rate,
  seeds, CPU/float32 route, batch and epoch budgets, patience, prohibited
  features, and checkpoint/threshold direction to exact executable values.
- Freeze the remaining input order, normalization, mask behavior,
  initialization, deterministic settings, threshold/checkpoint tie-breaks,
  collapse and metric implementations, replay tolerances, exception handling,
  artifact schemas, and terminal decision rules without changing an approved
  value.
- Record known test exposure and prohibit test-driven rescue, best-seed
  selection, architecture shopping, or silent run replacement.

**Exit gate**

- A versioned, hash-bound protocol is internally consistent, executable, and
  approved before substantive training begins.

## Milestone 4 - Frozen training and checkpoint reconstruction

**State:** Pending.

**Deliverables**

- Train every predeclared seed under the same frozen budget.
- Preserve complete train and validation histories, run manifests, gradients,
  initial and final weight hashes, environment identity, durations, exceptions,
  and checkpoints.
- Reconstruct every selected checkpoint in a fresh process and prove the frozen
  replay requirement.

**Exit gate**

- Every declared seed has a retained terminal record: valid, failed, invalid,
  or interrupted with exact cause. No seed is silently replaced.
- No test evidence has been used to alter the protocol.

## Milestone 5 - One retrospective evaluation

**State:** Pending and unopened by Experiment Three.

**Deliverables**

- Open the known Experiment 1 test role once for all frozen seeds.
- Compare every seed with the accepted RBR baseline, canonical U-Net, constant
  background, and constant burned controls under explicitly mapped metrics.
- Report per-event confusion, Dice, IoU, worst-event performance, predicted-
  burn prevalence, probabilities, collapse gates, exclusions, and deviations.
- Assign `lifecycle_status` and `comparative_status` without tuning or rescue.

**Exit gate**

- Evaluation is reproducible from frozen inputs and checkpoints.
- All seeds and controls appear in the machine-readable and human-readable
  result, including unfavorable or invalid outcomes.
- Claims remain retrospective and sparse-core bounded.

## Milestone 6 - Reviewer evidence and terminal closeout

**State:** Pending.

**Deliverables**

- Publish architecture proof, training curves, per-seed/per-event comparison,
  probability maps, class maps, error maps, input/reference/control panels,
  model card, limitations, evidence manifest, and exact replay instructions.
- Mark unscored pixels and qualitative full-patch output unmistakably.
- Verify rendered surfaces, archive immutable evidence, reconcile all project
  records, and create one verified GitHub release from the reviewed revision.

**Exit gate**

- A clean local reconstruction reproduces required evidence within frozen
  tolerances.
- Repository records, release assets, tags, and public claims agree.
- The experiment has a terminal `PASS`, `FAIL`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `INVALID`
  comparative disposition and is not extended into post-test tuning.

## Deferred lane - Fresh-event confirmation

Fresh-event confirmation is not required for the primary experiment and is not
authorized by this roadmap. It would require an owner-approved cohort and
protocol, independently sampled and reviewed labels, blindness to RBR and model
outputs, sealed evidence, and one frozen opening. It must not become a covert
extension or rescue of Experiment Three.

## Continuing authority and stop boundaries

Within a frozen milestone and approved dependencies, routine reversible work
may continue through implementation, validation, versioning, commits, review,
merge, and verified publication. Stop for owner direction before changing any
scientific promise, claim boundary, model family, frozen protocol, cohort,
label, split, metric, decision rule, external-source terms, credentials,
spending, service, access, ownership, custody, another repository, irreversible
state, or unverified public claim.
