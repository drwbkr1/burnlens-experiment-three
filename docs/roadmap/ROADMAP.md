# BurnLens Experiment Three Roadmap

## Terminal outcome

Experiment Three will build and truthfully close one deliberately bounded
neural change-detection experiment. Completion requires a verified,
fresh-process-replayable lifecycle. It does not require favorable comparative
metrics.

The primary question is whether a 137-parameter pointwise neural detector can
avoid the canonical U-Net's constant-class behavior on the frozen Experiment 1
Deschutes retrospective compatibility benchmark.

Each cycle begins by reconciling repository, custody, runtime, model,
evaluation, release, and public-state truth. It then selects one bounded,
highest-leverage evidence unit from the first incomplete milestone.

## Deferred lane - Fresh-event confirmation

Fresh-event confirmation is not required for the primary experiment and is not
authorized by this roadmap. It would require an owner-approved cohort and
protocol, independently sampled and reviewed labels, blindness to RBR and model
outputs, sealed evidence, and one frozen opening. It must not become a covert
extension or rescue of Experiment Three.

## Continuing authority and stop boundaries

Within a frozen milestone and approved dependencies, routine reversible work
may continue through implementation, validation, versioning, commits, review,
merge, and verified publication. Stop for owner direction before changing any
scientific promise, claim boundary, model family, frozen protocol, cohort,
label, split, metric, decision rule, external-source terms, credentials,
spending, service, access, ownership, custody, another repository, irreversible
state, or unverified public claim.
